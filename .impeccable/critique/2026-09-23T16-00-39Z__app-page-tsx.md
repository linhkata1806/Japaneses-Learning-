## Overall Impression

The first question is approachable and the learning feedback is useful. The page loses its momentum after the single sample because the surrounding level selector implies a broader course, while the next action is unclear. The strongest opportunity is to make the beta's real scope unmistakable and guide learners to a useful continuation.

## What's Working

- The sample is transparent about its provenance and links to the source information page; it is not presented as official or reviewed JLPT material.
- Vietnamese feedback explains why an answer fits and gives a memory cue, so the exercise can teach after a wrong answer.
- Answer choices are labeled; checking is disabled until the learner chooses an option; retry is available after feedback.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Four locked levels dominate the level selector.** `app/page.tsx` renders N4–N1 as disabled buttons and repeats their unavailability nearby. This makes the only usable starting point feel like a restricted course and forces attention onto controls the learner cannot use. Show N5 as the active starting point, and summarize the planned progression in one concise status message. Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`.

2. **[P1] Completing the one sample does not lead to a next action.** The page labels the exercise as a sample and shows “Câu 1 / 1,” but completion can feel like reaching the end of a full set. Add an end state that names the sample as complete and offers a truthful next step, such as visiting the community library or creating a lesson from personal material. Do not imply these contain a full validated course. Suggested command: `$impeccable onboard`.

3. **[P2] Secondary content competes with the learning loop.** Navigation, instructional panels, progress, tips, and Tatoeba examples share the page with a single exercise. External example content extends the exercise card beyond answer and feedback. Keep the question and its explanation as the dominant sequence; group reference material under a clearly secondary section or link. Suggested command: `$impeccable layout`.

4. **[P2] “1/1” progress can overstate what the learner completed.** In the signed-out state, `1/1` can read as a finished practice set, even though it represents a single sample and the account link notes that progress is not saved until sign-in. Label the measure as completion of this sample and pair it with the next action. Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify`.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Jordan, first-timer:** The first action is clear, but four disabled level buttons imply that the learner should choose among unavailable material. After finishing the sample, there is no prominent continuation. Put the real beta scope and current next action before the learner meets the locked choices.
- **Alex, power user:** The answer loop is short, but it stops after one sample and offers no visible way to continue practice. Standard buttons support basic keyboard use; keyboard shortcuts are absent.
- **Sam, accessibility-dependent learner:** Text accompanies the answer-state colors, but focus visibility, contrast, mobile reflow, and announcement of result/save states could not be checked in a rendered browser.

## Minor Observations

- Tatoeba examples provide author, source, and license links; their placement pulls focus from the sample question.
- The signed-out progress panel could state more clearly that attempts are not persisted until sign-in.
